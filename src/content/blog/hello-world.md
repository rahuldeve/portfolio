This is my corner of the internet. I'm a data scientist, and most of my work happens inside companies and labs where it's hard to show anyone. This blog is where I'll write about it anyway.

Expect posts on:

- building ML models that hold up outside the notebook,
- LLM agents that do real work, like generating plots from a question,
- fairness and bias in machine learning, which I researched at Rutgers.

To add a post, drop a Markdown file into `src/content/blog/` with a `title`, `description` and `date` at the top. That's it.
